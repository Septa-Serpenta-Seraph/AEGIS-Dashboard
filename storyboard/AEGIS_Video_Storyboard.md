## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Vertical format (9:16)** works better for Twitter/X. We can shoot exterior shots in portrait.
- **Color grading:** Cool blues for tech scenes, warm gold for human scenes.
- **Pacing:** Keep cuts tight, especially in first 15 seconds.
- **Voiceover:** Record in a quiet room, normalize audio, add slight compression.
- **Editing:** Use InShot (Adora’s phone) or DaVinci Resolve (free desktop).
